# HANDOFF.md

## Project Overview

**Project:** Niche Clothing Brand  
**Style:** Minimalist Pixel Art + Streetwear

---

## 1. User Problem

Online clothing shoppers often struggle with:

- **Size and fit uncertainty**
- Understanding **sizing accuracy**
- Finding products through **difficult navigation**

---

## 2. Target Audience

- **Teens and young adults**
- People interested in **unique streetwear**
- Shoppers who like **pixel-art and digital aesthetics**
- People looking for clothing that feels **different from mainstream brands**

---

## 3. Aesthetic Theme

### Minimalist Pixel Art

- Clean and simple layouts
- Pixel-art graphics and icons
- Limited color palette
- Modern streetwear feel
- Easy-to-read typography
- Pixel-art elements should add personality without making the site difficult to use

### Color Theme

- Claude-inspired palette: ivory background, near-black text and navigation bar, warm orange accent
- All colors are stored as CSS variables in the `:root` block of `css/style.css`

### Imagery

- Pixel art is used for the interface only (header, icons, buttons)
- Product images are realistic clothing photos, not pixel art

---

## 4. Interface Goals

### Navigation

Use **5 main navigation sections**:

1. **Home** — Main landing page and featured products
2. **Shop** — Browse all clothing/products
3. **Collections** — Browse products by collection or category
4. **About** — Brand story and identity
5. **Cart** — Review items and proceed to checkout

### Usability

- Make products **easy to find**
- Make product details and sizing **easy to understand**
- Make purchasing **simple and straightforward**
- Keep navigation **clear and consistent**
- Use a **sticky header** so the navigation bar stays visible while scrolling, and a section reached from a nav link is never hidden under it
- Make the website usable on both **desktop and mobile**

### Branding

- Create a **strong and recognizable brand identity**
- Use minimalist pixel art consistently across the website
- Keep the visual style unique while maintaining good usability
